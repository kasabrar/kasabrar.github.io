Subtopic 1: Teletherapy

Link to Cite Adrian Aguilera
Abstract This article explores the transformative impact of digital technologies—such as the internet, mobile devices, and social media—on mental health interventions. Aguilera discusses how these technologies have democratized access to information and opened new avenues for delivering mental health services. The paper highlights applications like mobile health (mHealth) tools and online therapy platforms that offer real-time data collection and personalized support. It also examines challenges, including privacy concerns, ethical considerations, and the need for evidence-based validation of digital interventions. The author emphasizes the importance of integrating technology thoughtfully into mental health care to enhance accessibility and effectiveness.
Link to Cite Christopher A. Lovejoy
Abstract This article discusses the transformative potential of artificial intelligence (AI) in the field of psychiatry. It emphasizes how AI, particularly through Natural Language Processing (NLP), can alleviate clinicians' workloads by efficiently analyzing extensive textual data, such as patient notes. This efficiency allows psychiatrists to dedicate more time to direct patient interactions, potentially enhancing the quality of care. The piece underscores the significance of integrating AI tools to support mental health professionals in diagnosis and treatment planning, while also acknowledging the necessity for careful implementation to address ethical and practical considerations.
